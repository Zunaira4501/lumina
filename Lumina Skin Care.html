The Skincare Dilemma

You shouldn't need a chemistry degree to buy moisturizer.

Can't pronounce half the ingredients?

Cosmetic brands hide pore-clogging chemicals, drying alcohols, and allergens behind chemical synonyms like Isopropyl Myristate or Sodium Lauryl Sulfate.

Worried about pore-clogging products?

Many "gentle" or "organic" creams contain highly comedogenic oils. They trigger deep cystic breakouts without your knowledge.

Tired of invasive databases selling your data?

Most scanner apps force you to sign up, sync contacts, and upload private facial photos to remote servers just to analyze a simple lotion.
